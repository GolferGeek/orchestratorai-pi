This synthetic document is for Pi smoke tests only. It contains no client information.

The parties may exchange confidential information to evaluate a possible business relationship. Confidential information includes marked information and information a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. The receiving party may use it only for the evaluation and may disclose it to representatives with a need to know.

The agreement lasts two years, and confidentiality obligations survive for three years after each disclosure. Upon request, the receiving party will return or destroy the information, except for copies retained by law or automatic backup systems.
