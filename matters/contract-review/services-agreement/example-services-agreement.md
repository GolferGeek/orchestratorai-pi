## 1. Services

Provider will perform the professional services described in one or more Statements of Work (“SOWs”). Each SOW is incorporated by reference. If an SOW conflicts with this Agreement, the SOW controls for that engagement only as to scope, fees, and timeline; this Agreement controls on all other terms.

Customer will pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt. Late amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month. Provider may suspend services after ten (10) days’ written notice of nonpayment.

EXCEPT FOR BREACH OF CONFIDENTIALITY OR IP INFRINGEMENT INDEMNITY (IF ANY), EACH PARTY’S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID BY CUSTOMER IN THE THREE (3) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM. NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES.

## 7. Indemnity

Customer will indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising from Customer materials or Customer’s misuse of Deliverables. The Agreement is silent on Provider indemnity for IP infringement arising from Deliverables.

## 8. Term and termination

This Agreement continues until terminated. Either party may terminate for material breach if uncured within thirty (30) days after written notice. Customer may terminate for convenience on thirty (30) days’ notice and will pay for work performed through the effective date.
