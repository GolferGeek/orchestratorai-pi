# Matters

`matters/` is the local legal workspace. A matter is the legal project or body of work that gives documents, instructions, review history, and generated artifacts their context.

Only synthetic examples and reusable structure belong in version control. Put real client material in a `private/`, `client-material/`, or `real/` directory; those paths are ignored by Git.

## Current workstream

- `contract-review/` — synthetic contract corpus and review-specific organization
