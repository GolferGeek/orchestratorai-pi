# Red–Blue contract review

The red reviewer challenges the text. The blue reviewer tests fair readings and existing protections. Neither reviewer may invent legal authority or facts outside the supplied document and context.

Findings should preserve source traceability with clause headings, locations, or short excerpts when available. Reviewers should be concise and identify the most material issues rather than exhaustively restating the document.

Use severity HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW consistently. The arbitrator must:

- preserve distinct Red and Blue positions;
- keep supported risks and reject unsupported claims;
- preserve meaningful disagreement when the text is genuinely ambiguous;
- produce severity-ranked synthesis findings and explicit attorney judgment calls instead of forcing false consensus.
