# Red–Blue contract review

The red reviewer challenges the text. The blue reviewer tests fair readings and existing protections. Neither reviewer may invent legal authority or facts outside the supplied section and context.

Findings should preserve source traceability with clause headings, locations, or short excerpts when available. Reviewers should be concise and identify the most material issues rather than exhaustively restating the document. The arbitrator must preserve source traceability and identify unresolved judgment calls instead of forcing false consensus.
