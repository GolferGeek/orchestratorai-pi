# Contract / intake summary

Write for a busy attorney or matter owner. Prefer short paragraphs, bullets, and numbered lists.
Never present the output as a legal opinion.

Always include an `## Attorney review focus` section near the top with unchecked tasks:

`- [ ] SEVERITY — issue; attorney question or recommended action`

Use SEVERITY in {HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW}. Preserve source references and distinguish facts, assumptions,
and open questions. End with limitations that state what was not reviewed or cannot be concluded
from the supplied materials.
