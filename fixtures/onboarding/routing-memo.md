# Legal Intake Memorandum

**Prepared for:** General Counsel  
**Date:** August 12, 2026  
**Subject:** Proposed acquisition of Cedar Analytics

This memorandum summarizes the initial business rationale for a possible acquisition of Cedar Analytics.
It is not an agreement and does not create obligations for either party.

## Initial concerns

- Cedar’s customer contracts may require consent before a change of control.
- The diligence team has not yet received the employee invention-assignment schedule.
- The target’s privacy disclosures should be compared with its actual data practices.

## Proposed next steps

Create a diligence workstream, request the missing schedules, and route the customer-contract questions to
the M&A and privacy review workflows.
