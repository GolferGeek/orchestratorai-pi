# Office Lease

This Office Lease is entered into on August 1, 2026, by Oak Street Properties, LLC (“Landlord”) and
Northstar Consulting, Inc. (“Tenant”).

## Premises and term

Landlord leases Suite 400 at 18 Oak Street, Chicago, Illinois. The term begins September 1, 2026 and ends
August 31, 2029.

## Rent

Monthly base rent is $8,500, due on the first day of each month. Late charges are 5% of unpaid rent after a
five-day grace period.

## Use and maintenance

Tenant may use the premises for general office purposes. Landlord will maintain the building structure and
common areas. Tenant will maintain the interior of the premises.

## Insurance and termination

Tenant will maintain commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence. Either
party may terminate for a material uncured breach after thirty days’ written notice.
