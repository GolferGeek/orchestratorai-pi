# Mutual Confidentiality Agreement

**Version date:** March 1, 2026  
**Effective date:** February 15, 2026

This Mutual Confidentiality Agreement is entered into by Aspen Devices, Inc. (“Aspen”) and Harbor Peak
Systems, LLC (“Harbor Peak”).

## Purpose

The parties may exchange information to evaluate a proposed commercial relationship.

## Confidential information

Confidential information includes information marked confidential and information that should reasonably be
understood to be confidential.

## Term

The confidentiality obligations continue for three years after disclosure. The opening page of the draft
refers to a two-year confidentiality period, but that provision is not included in this copy.

## Permitted disclosures

Information may be shared with representatives who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality duties.

## Related documents

This agreement refers to a “Data Room Protocol” and a “Schedule 1,” neither of which is attached.
