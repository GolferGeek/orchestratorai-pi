# Ruling - Meridian Analytics v. Corvid Labs (9th Cir., Sept 8 2026)

**Type:** ruling  **Jurisdiction:** Ninth Circuit (federal)  **Practice area:** trade secrets, commercial contracts

The Ninth Circuit narrowed the scope of residuals clauses, holding that a clause licensing "unaided memory" does not reach a design that a party reconstructed with the help of contemporaneous notes, and that the burden of showing the memory was unaided sits with the party invoking the clause. The court expressly declined to decide whether a residuals licence can bar a DTSA claim.
