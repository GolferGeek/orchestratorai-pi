# Regulatory guidance - Valuation refresh expectations (Sept 4 2026)

**Type:** guidance  **Jurisdiction:** US (federal)  **Practice area:** corporate, tax

Guidance states that a valuation more than 12 months old, or one predating a material event, will not be presumed reasonable for equity grants. Grants made on a stale valuation may be treated as discounted options with the associated penalties for the grantee.
