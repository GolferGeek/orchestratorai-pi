# Data Processing Addendum - Cedar Analytics, Inc.

**Synthetic fixture for demonstration. All parties, facts, figures and citations are fictional.**

**Applies to:** all customer agreements incorporating this DPA by reference. **Version:** 1.0, dated January 2023.

## 2. Roles
Customer is the controller; Cedar is the processor.

## 4. Sub-processors
Cedar may engage sub-processors with **10 days' notice** and the customer may object. The current sub-processor list names four vendors, **two of which (an analytics vendor in India and a support vendor in the Philippines) were added in 2025 and do not appear in the version of the list attached to the Northwind MSA.**

## 6. International transfers
Transfers out of the EEA rely on **the EU-US Privacy Shield framework.** No standard contractual clauses are attached to this DPA.

## 8. Security
Cedar shall maintain "industry standard" security measures. No specific controls, encryption standards, or audit rights are specified. **No SOC 2 report is in the data room; a memo states one is "in progress".**

## 9. Breach notification
Cedar shall notify the customer of a personal data breach "without undue delay". No hour-count is specified.

## 11. Deletion
On termination Cedar shall delete customer personal data within 90 days, **except that backups are retained for 24 months and are expressly excluded from deletion requests.**
