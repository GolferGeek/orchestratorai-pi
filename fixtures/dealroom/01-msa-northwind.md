## 7. Service Levels

## 11. Assignment and Change of Control
Neither party may assign this Agreement, by operation of law or otherwise, **including in connection with a merger, consolidation, or sale of substantially all assets or equity, without the other party's prior written consent, which may be withheld in its sole discretion.** Any purported assignment without such consent is void and constitutes a material breach.

## 12. Most Favoured Customer
Cedar warrants that Northwind's per-seat pricing is no less favourable than that offered to any other customer of comparable volume. Cedar shall promptly refund the difference if this is breached.

## 16. Limitation of Liability
Neither party's aggregate liability shall exceed fees paid in the preceding 12 months, **except for breaches of confidentiality, indemnity obligations, and Section 12, which are uncapped.**

## 19. Governing Law
Laws of the State of New York. Exclusive jurisdiction in New York County.
