# Harbor Peak Systems — Data Handling and Security Policy (v2.1, synthetic demo)

**This document is a synthetic fixture for demonstration only.**

## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy governs how Harbor Peak Systems, LLC ("the Company") collects, stores, processes, and shares personal data of customers, employees, and partners, and the security controls that protect it. It applies to all employees and contractors.

## 2. Data collection and lawful basis
The Company collects personal data necessary to deliver its data-integration services. Customer contact data is collected at account creation. Marketing communications are sent to all customers unless they opt out. The Company does not currently document a lawful basis per processing activity.

## 3. Data retention
Customer data is retained for the life of the account plus seven years. Employee data is retained indefinitely. Backups are retained for two years and are not covered by deletion requests.

## 4. Data subject requests
Customers may email privacy@harborpeak.example to request a copy of their data. Requests are handled by the support team within 60 days. There is no process for rectification, erasure, or portability requests, and no verification of requester identity beyond the email address.

## 5. Access controls
Production systems require a username and password. Multi-factor authentication is encouraged for administrators. Access reviews are performed annually. Shared service accounts are used by the integration team for convenience.

## 6. Encryption
Data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Data at rest in the primary database is encrypted; data in analytics exports and backups is not.

## 7. Vendors and international transfers
Customer data is processed by cloud and analytics vendors in the United States and India. Vendor contracts include confidentiality clauses. No data processing agreements or transfer mechanisms are maintained.

## 8. Security incidents and breach notification
Employees report suspected incidents to IT. IT investigates and, if a breach is confirmed, notifies affected customers "as soon as practical." There is no defined regulator-notification timeline and no incident log.

## 9. Training and governance
New hires receive a security briefing. The Head of Engineering is responsible for this policy. There is no data protection officer and no periodic policy review schedule.

## 10. Financial reporting controls
Not applicable — the Company is privately held.
